## Full description

The chihuahua in a wig, the staring cat, Pietro the pigeon in sneakers, the pig
with a gold chain... and 20 more iconic memes become the heroes of a cute 2D
pixel-art platformer!

**24 PLAYABLE MEMES**
Every meme has a passive perk and a special move true to the joke: "I want to
speak to the manager!", the stare that freezes enemies, the police car that runs
everything over, Pietro's phone call, the egg bomb, HESOYAM and many more.

**500 LEVELS IN 10 WORLDS**
The Feed, the Comment Section, Reel Beach, Like Desert, Cringe Glacier, Trend
City, Meme Forest, Flame Volcano, Viral Clouds and the Server. Springs, moving
platforms, spikes, surprise blocks and power-ups ("Deal with it" shades, coffee,
Stonks, pizza). Every level is verified to be beatable.

**ENEMIES AND BOSSES**
Normies, Cringes, Haters throwing RATIOs and Boomers to stomp, and The Algorithm
itself at the end of each world.

**LOTS TO UNLOCK**
- 16 hats to buy with the likes you collect
- 17 trophies
- Meme of the day with double likes
- Online leaderboard for every level

**MADE FOR PHONES**
Big touch controls with a slide-able d-pad, haptics, a camera that looks ahead and
polished game feel in every jump. Plays offline too.

No ads. No in-app purchases.
