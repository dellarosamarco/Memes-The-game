## Full description

The chihuahua in a wig, the staring cat, Pietro the pigeon in sneakers, the pig
with a gold chain... and 20 more iconic memes face off in a cute pixel-art
platform fighter, Super Smash Bros style!

**MORE DAMAGE, BIGGER LAUNCH**
Rack up your opponent's damage percentage, then blast them off the stage with a
charged smash. Ground and air attacks, shield, dodges, double jump and recovery:
simple controls, plenty of depth.

**24 MEMES, 24 STYLES**
Every meme has a passive perk and a special move true to the joke: "I want to
speak to the manager!", the paralysing stare, the police car, Pietro's phone
call, the egg bomb, the purse smack, HESOYAM and many more.

**FREE FIGHT AND ARCADE**
Pick your opponent, the stage and the CPU level (Easy, Normal, Hard), or take on
Arcade: 8 opponents in a row, each tougher than the last.

**10 STAGES**
From the Feed Field and the Comment Section to the Flame Crater, the Viral Clouds
and the Data Center.

**PLENTY TO UNLOCK**
- 16 hats to buy with the likes you earn fighting
- 16 trophies
- Meme of the day with double likes
- Arcade records for every meme

**MADE FOR PHONES**
Big joystick and buttons, haptics, dynamic camera, hit-stop and screen shake on
every big hit. Plays fully offline.

No ads. No in-app purchases. No data collected.

*Memes: the game is a parody game: it is not affiliated with or endorsed by the
authors of the original images or the owners of the characters referenced.*
